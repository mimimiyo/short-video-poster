Privacy Policy — Short Video Poster

Last updated: 2026-09-26

Short Video Poster ("the App") is a private desktop tool operated by its developer to publish the developer's own

content to the developer's own TikTok accounts.

Information we access

When you sign in with TikTok, the App receives an access token and your basic profile information

(open ID, display name, avatar) and creator information needed to publish (nickname, allowed privacy levels).

How we use it

Only to publish videos and photos you choose to TikTok and to confirm which account is being used.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and a post log are stored only on the developer's local computer. We do not sell, share, or transfer any

information to third parties, and we do not collect information about other TikTok users.

Deleting your data

You can revoke access at any time from TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Local tokens are

deleted when access is revoked or on request.

Contact

Please send a direct message on TikTok to the account that published the content.
